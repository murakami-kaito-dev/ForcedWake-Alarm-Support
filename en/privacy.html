Privacy Policy

ForcedWake Alarm — Last updated: February 2025

Overview

ForcedWake Alarm ("the App") is developed by ForcedWake Studio ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. Specifically:

  • No personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No browsing or search history
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking sent to external servers

Camera Usage

The App uses your device's camera to detect exercise movements (squats, push-ups, burpees) and objects (books, pens) for alarm dismissal.

  • All camera processing is performed entirely on your device using on-device machine learning (Google ML Kit).
  • No images, video, or camera data are ever recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.
  • Camera data is processed in real-time and immediately discarded after analysis.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted:

  • Alarm settings: alarm time, mission type, sound preferences, volume
  • Exercise history: daily mission results (success/failure), streak counts
  • Achievement badges: badge unlock dates
  • App preferences: language setting

This data is stored using your device's local storage (SharedPreferences and SQLite) and can be deleted by uninstalling the App.

In-App Purchases

The App offers auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly and yearly plans) through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. We do not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party frameworks for on-device processing only:

  • Google ML Kit (Pose Detection, Image Labeling, Object Detection): All processing occurs on-device. No data is sent to Google servers.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since we do not collect any personal data from any user, there is no risk of children's data being collected.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

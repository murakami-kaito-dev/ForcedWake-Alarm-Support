Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The alarm didn't ring. What happened?

A: ForcedWake Alarm requires the app to remain open (not in the background). Make sure you tap "Good Night" and leave the app on screen. The alarm will not ring if the app is closed or running in the background.

Q: The camera isn't detecting my exercises.

A: Make sure your full body (for squats/burpees) or upper body (for push-ups) is visible in the camera. Place your phone at a distance where the camera can see your movements clearly. Good lighting also helps detection accuracy. If detection still isn't working, you can use the "Skip Detection" button.

Q: Can I lower the alarm volume?

A: On the free plan, the alarm always plays at maximum volume. Premium subscribers can adjust the alarm volume in the sound settings screen.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: Go to your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions > ForcedWake Alarm > Cancel Subscription. Your premium features will remain active until the end of the current billing period.

Q: How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

A: Open the app, go to the Premium screen, and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Q: What happens if I don't complete the exercise?

A: The alarm will automatically stop after 10 minutes as a safety measure. The day will be recorded as a failed mission, and your streak will be affected.

Q: Does the app send my camera data to any server?

A: No. All camera processing happens entirely on your device. No images or video are ever sent, stored, or shared with anyone.

System Requirements

  • iOS 12.0 or later
  • iPhone with front and rear cameras
  • Camera permission required for exercise detection

Subscription Information

  • ForcedWake Alarm offers monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions.
  • The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial.
  • Payment is charged to your iTunes account at confirmation of purchase.
  • Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage and cancel subscriptions in your iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.

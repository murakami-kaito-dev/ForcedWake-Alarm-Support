ForcedWake Alarm

The alarm clock that won't stop until you exercise. Build a healthy morning routine with AI-powered motion detection.

Features

🏋️

AI Exercise Detection

Real-time pose detection counts your squats, push-ups, and burpees using your camera. No wearables needed.

📖

Study Missions

Point your camera at a book or pen. The alarm stops only after you've spent time reading or studying.

🔊

Unstoppable Alarm

The alarm plays at maximum volume and cannot be silenced until you complete your mission.

🔥

Streaks & Badges

Track your consecutive wake-up days and earn achievement badges at 3, 7, 14, 50, and 100-day milestones.

📊

Statistics

View your history with a calendar, success rates, and mission breakdowns to stay motivated.

🎵

Custom Alarm Sounds

Choose from preset sounds or use your own music from your device library.

How It Works

1

Set Your Alarm

Choose your wake-up time and select a mission: workout or study.

2

Go to Sleep

Tap "Good Night" to enter sleep mode. Keep the app open.

3

Wake Up & Move

When the alarm rings, exercise in front of your camera to dismiss it.

4

Start Your Day

Mission complete! You're awake, energized, and ready to go.

Privacy

Your privacy matters. All camera processing happens entirely on your device using on-device AI. No images or video are ever sent to any server, stored, or shared. Exercise data is saved locally on your device only.

2. Overview of the Service

ForcedWake Alarm is a subscription-based alarm app designed to help users build better morning habits. Key features include:

  • Alarm: Sounds an alarm at your set time to wake you up.
  • Mission-based dismissal: To stop the alarm, you must complete a mission verified by the camera — such as exercise (squats, push-ups, burpees), reading, or studying. The alarm cannot be dismissed by simply pressing snooze.
  • Habit tracking: Records your mission history and streaks.

5. Camera Usage

The App uses your device's camera to verify mission completion.

  • Exercise detection: The camera detects physical movements such as squats, push-ups, and burpees.
  • Reading & studying detection: The camera detects objects such as books and pens.
  • Data handling: All camera processing is performed entirely on your device using on-device AI (Google ML Kit) in real time. No images, video, or camera data are ever transmitted to, recorded by, or stored on any external server.

If you deny camera access, missions that require camera detection will not be available.
